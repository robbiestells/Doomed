How these numbers are built

Outages come mainly from news headlines, grouped by company and day, with commentary, officials' reactions and roundups removed. "Is X down?" pages count as evidence but not as articles. Status-page incidents and a small manual log fill in the rest. Causes and AI links are read from headlines and update text by keyword, so each is a first pass.

Scope: outages that touch daily or work life, such as banks, airlines, government, consumer apps, commerce and the SaaS tools people work in. Developer-only infrastructure is kept only when the press covered it widely. Outages of AI companies' own products (Anthropic, OpenAI and similar) are left out unless coverage shows another industry, such as finance or travel, was hit.

Press events before Nov 12 come from a monthly news search that returns only about 10 to 20 headlines per query, so it finds the big outages and misses smaller ones. The record on the sign counts only the period since daily collection began.
